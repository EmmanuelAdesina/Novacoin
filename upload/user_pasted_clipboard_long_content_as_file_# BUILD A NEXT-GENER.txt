# BUILD A NEXT-GENERATION PROTOTYPE FOR A MASS-MARKET NIGERIAN DIGITAL ASSET NETWORK

## THE IDEA — UNDERSTAND THIS FIRST

We are exploring a new consumer digital-asset network designed primarily for **ordinary Nigerians**, especially young, mobile-first users.

The concept is simple:

A person can open the app, buy the network's digital asset with Nigerian naira, hold it, watch its value change, send it to another person, receive it, and eventually sell it back.

The long-term vision is much bigger than a coin.

We are trying to create a **large social and economic network around one digital asset**.

Think:

**Buy → Hold → Send → Receive → Use → Sell**

The product should make this incredibly easy.

The user should NOT need to understand blockchain.

They should NOT need to understand wallets, gas fees, smart contracts, bridges, chains, RPCs, seed phrases, or technical crypto terminology.

The technology should disappear behind an incredibly simple consumer experience.

---

# THE MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCT IDEA

This is **not an enterprise fintech dashboard.**

This is **not a corporate treasury platform.**

This is **not a serious-looking banking app.**

This is **not a developer tool.**

This is **not a crypto exchange clone.**

This is a **fun, social, consumer digital-asset product**.

Imagine:

**CoinGecko + Robinhood-style simplicity + social network energy + modern gaming UX + Nigerian culture**

But do NOT literally copy any existing company's interface, branding, illustrations, or layout.

We want something original.

The emotional reaction should be:

> "Wait... this is actually really cool."

Not:

> "This looks like another boring financial dashboard."

---

Before considering the prototype complete, ask:

### Could a Nigerian university student understand this without knowing crypto?

### Would they actually want to show the app to their friend?

### Does it feel fun enough to open repeatedly?

### Does the interface communicate that thousands/millions of people could participate?

### Does it feel premium enough to become a major consumer brand?

### Does it feel Nigerian without looking small or local?

### Does it feel like a financial product without feeling boring?

### Can someone understand BUY / SELL / SEND / RECEIVE immediately?

### Does the product make the concept of a "network" obvious?

### Does it look dramatically better than a generic crypto wallet?

If the answer to any of these is no, continue refining.
